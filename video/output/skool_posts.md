## 4. Japan 1989 (day 3)

**Title:** Your world ETF has been this concentrated once before

**Body:**

In 1989, Japan made up around 42% of the entire global stock market, at about 60 times earnings. If you bought "the world", you mostly bought Japan.

The Nikkei took 34 years, until February 2024, to get back to that level. But the world index, in dollars, was back within about four years, because as Japan shrank the index automatically shifted to everything else.

Today the USA is more than 70% of MSCI World.

## 6. The euro trap (day 5)

**Title:** Your US stock didn't move. You still lost 9%.

**Body:**

Say an American stock stays exactly flat in dollars for a year. If the dollar falls against the euro, you just lost about 9% in euros.

Apple is a dollar company whether you buy it in Frankfurt, Milan or New York. And with more than 70% of a world ETF in the USA, the currency matters more than most people think.
